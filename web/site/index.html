Gestures

Pen: draw. Pen eraser end: erase. Hold the pen still at the end of a line to snap it straight or into a shape.

Two fingers: move, zoom and turn. Tap with two fingers to undo, three to redo.

Keyboard: B paint, S smudge, E erase, I eyedropper, G fill, M select, V move, U shapes, R ruler, Ctrl+' grid, [ and ] size, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Space and drag to move.

Selections: Ctrl+A all, Ctrl+D deselect, Ctrl+Shift+I invert, Delete erases, Shift adds, Alt takes away. Transform: arrow keys nudge, Enter finishes, Esc cancels.

Painting on the mask: black hides, white shows.
